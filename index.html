🏢 Our Story

Rinaha International Limited

Founded in August 2018 and headquartered in Kampala, Uganda, Rinaha International Limited is a registered fruit and vegetable export company serving Uganda, South Sudan, the Middle East, and Europe.

Committed to supplying high-quality organic produce from Uganda's naturally fertile soils. Fully compliant with national regulatory requirements, globally certified, and affiliated with UEPB and UFVEPA.

🎯 Our Mission

To build long-term relationships and provide exceptional service through innovation and technology.
